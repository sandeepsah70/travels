A "Group Booking" is defined as a reservation of ten (10) or more passengers traveling together on the same flight itinerary on at least one flight segment, as governed by airline group tariffs and standard airline group regulations.

3. Payment Schedules and Deadlines

Group booking contracts adhere to strict airline-mandated milestone schedules:
